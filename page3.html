PC building

Building a PC is a complicated process that can be overwhelming for beginners, many dont know how or when to start assembling one.

Parts

The main parts of a PC that you need are the case, motherboard, CPU, CPU cooler, Thermal Paste, RAM, storage, power supply, and any additional components you want to include. Make sure you have everything you need in order to start.

Assembly

Once you have all the parts, you can start assembling your PC.

  • Start by prepping the Motherboard, lay the component on its box, press down on the retention mechanism for the CPU socket and open the latch. Place the CPU in the socket with the highlighted corner going in the marked spot on your motherboard. MAKE SURE TO HOLD THE CPU BY THE EDGES AND NOT THE PINS, if you have to set the CPU down for any reason, put it back it it's clampshell
  • you have the CPU seated, close the latch and press down the latch to secure it, It will be an uncomfortable ammount of pressure. That is expected
  • Once the CPU is secure you can go ahead and install your mounting brackets for your CPU Cooler. Once those are installed, you can then go over to the ram. Go to the back of the RAM package and look for the timings, then go to your motherboard book and check the timings there, They should match. Once that's confirmed you should locate the 2nd adn 4th slots on the Motherboard, insert until you hear a click.
  • Next once you are done RAM we can head back to the CPU Cooler, do a big blog of thermal paste in the center if an AMD cpu or a Line down the middle if an intel CPU. Align the Cooler with the mounting brackets, Use pictures of your CPU Cooler and the mounting brackets to ensure proper alignment if you need help. Push down and screw it.
  • Once the CPU cooler is intalled we will insert the Motherboard inside the case, align the 9 holes on the motherboard with the 9 holes on the case, and add screws on all of them, make sure to evenly tighten all screws to prevent cracking of the Motherboard. after that lay the case on it's side with the bothboard facing up as before. uncrew the sheild sligned with the following and go ahead and open the GPU bracket. Make sure you are using the TOP most PCIe slot and push down with your GPU, go ahead and reuse the screw for the shield to install the GPU securely.
  • Once that's going, install your Drive, this will be an M.2 NVME drive, Take off whatever sheilding there is if there is any on your motherboard and instert the m.2 drive at a 45 degree angle, push until the connector is no more, press down your drive until parallel with your board and then screw it into your motherboard in the included notch.
  • Next we prepare your power supply unit (psu), you'll want to line up all the cables to each of their slot, 6 pin for gpu, 6pin for cpu, 24pin for motherboard, so on and so forth. After that place your psu in the alcove in the back and screw it in to the case, after that, route your cables to their respective homes, Next up is Headers.
