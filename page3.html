PC building

Building a PC is a complicated process that can be overwhelming for beginners, many dont know how or when to start assembling one.

Parts

The main parts of a PC that you need are the case, motherboard, CPU, CPU cooler, Thermal Paste, RAM, storage, power supply, and any additional components you want to include. Make sure you have everything you need in order to start.

Assembly

Once you have all the parts, you can start assembling your PC.

  1. Start by prepping the Motherboard, lay the component on its box, press down on the retention mechanism for the CPU socket and open the latch. Place the CPU in the socket with the highlighted corner going in the marked spot on your motherboard. MAKE SURE TO HOLD THE CPU BY THE EDGES AND NOT THE PINS, if you have to set the CPU down for any reason, put it back it it's clampshell
  2. Once you have the CPU seated, close the latch and press down the latch to secure it, It will be an uncomforable ammount of pressure. That is expected
  3. Once the CPU is secure you can go ahead and install your mounting brackets for your CPU Cooler, there should be directions included. Once those are installed, you can then go over to the ram. Go to the back of the RAM package and look for the timings, then go to your motherboard book and check the timings there, They should match. Once that's confirmed you should open 1 of the 2 latches on the motherboard RAM slots... Specifcaly 2 and 4.
  4. Next once you are done RAM we can head back to the CPU Cooler, Align the Cooler with the mounting brackets, Use pictures of your CPU Cooler and the mounting brackets to ensure proper alignment if you need help,
  5. The storage goes in the DIMM slots next to the CPU slots, it is outlined in the case you ordered, make sure to open the little plastic tabs, put the storage inside, and close it until you hear a click
  6. The power supply goes in the bottom rear of the case, screw it into the wall of the case
  7. The graphics card (GPU) goes in the PCIe slot, make sure to align it correctly and secure it with screws in the case outlined in the longest horizontal slot
  8. The case fans go on the front or back of the case, even the side, and plug it into the SYS_FAN or CHA_FAN connectors of the motherboard

If you ordered a Snowblind case however, here are the steps

  1. Start by putting the display case you should have received, and mount it into your PC case
  2. Install the leds by connecting the cables using the built in power cable located in the rear compartment
  3. Now lastly,connect the display controller to your PC

    4. And you are finished!
