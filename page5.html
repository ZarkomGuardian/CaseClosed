Taking care of your PC

Either you are getting a PC, or already have one, it is important to know these things in order for your PC to perform well, and remain clean while working properly working, follow these procedures to maintain your PC

  • When your computer is getting dusty (and it will), please shutdown your PC, take a can of compressed air, and hold the fans in imminent blast radius to prevent backfeeding and wear and tear on the fan's motor.
  • Using software such as Armory Crate for AMD or the Nvidia App, if you notice your CPU temps are unusally high while underload or even worse, while on just desktop.
