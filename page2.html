Why we made this site

The problem

PC's are a complicated, but rewarding process when it comes to assembling, and we always wished there was an easier way, to customize, to understand, or just build to get it done

The idea

And one day, instead of talking, we acted, we did not want to wait for someone to do it for us, and it was the best idea to do it ourselves, so our team decided to start make this website, to help people like us, who were struggling with getting into PC assembling when they dont know what to start with

Problems

It was first an informational website, but the lack of ideas and wants we needed was just not enough, and then we realized, what about PC cases?

The Final Product

We decided to make a website where PC cases are the matter, that despite at an affordable price, it is still a very high quality meant for the best performance possible. But that is not it, because it is also meant to help people assemble their PC's

We feel proud of the result that this project ended up being, that now people can find a trustworthy website for them to count on when it comes to their needs

The Future of Case Closed

There is one thing we look forward to fix, and it is the lack of variaty, as you know, Case Closed is a website meant to help assemble and buy PC cases (customized, snowball, etc) But we want to expand on our creation, to not only making it PC cases, but an entire component shop in general, and help people the way we truly wanted to since day 1.
